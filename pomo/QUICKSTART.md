# Quick Start Guide - Alpine Wildflower Garden

## For iPhone (Recommended) 📱

### Step 1: Deploy to GitHub Pages
1. Create a new repository on GitHub
2. Upload all 9 files from the `pomodoro-garden` folder
3. Go to Settings → Pages → Select "main" branch → Save
4. Copy your GitHub Pages URL (e.g., `https://yourname.github.io/pomodoro-garden/`)

### Step 2: Install on iPhone
1. Open **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome!)
2. Go to your GitHub Pages URL
3. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Give it a name like "Alpine Garden"
6. Tap "Add"

🎉 Done! You now have a beautiful app icon on your home screen!

## For Windows Desktop 💻

### Quick Test (No Installation)
1. Open the `pomodoro-garden` folder
2. Double-click `index.html`
3. It will open in your browser!

### Better Experience (Local Server)
1. Open Command Prompt or PowerShell
2. Navigate to the folder: `cd path\to\pomodoro-garden`
3. Run: `python -m http.server 8000`
4. Open browser to: `http://localhost:8000`

## How It Works

### Growing Your First Alpine Wildflower 🌱

1. Click **"Start Growing 🌱"**
2. Work for 25 minutes
3. Watch your alpine wildflower grow in 5 stages:
   - Stage 1 (0-5 min): Seed sprouts from mountain soil
   - Stage 2 (5-10 min): Small stem + leaves appear
   - Stage 3 (10-15 min): Taller stem grows
   - Stage 4 (15-20 min): Bud forms
   - Stage 5 (20-25 min): Full alpine bloom! 🌸

4. When timer completes:
   - Flower celebration animation! 🎉
   - Flower auto-saves to your garden
   - Time for a 5-minute break 🏔️

### Your Alpine Garden 🏔️

- Click the 🏡 button (top right) to see all your wildflowers
- Each flower shows the date you grew it
- Hover over flowers to see their names (Colorado Columbine, Indian Paintbrush, etc.)
- Your garden has a beautiful mountain backdrop!
- Your garden persists - flowers stay even after closing the app!
- Click ⏱️ to return to the timer

### Alpine Wildflowers You'll Grow

The app randomly selects from 8 different Colorado alpine wildflowers:
- Colorado Columbine (state flower!) with blue, white, and yellow
- Indian Paintbrush in vibrant reds
- Mountain Lupine with purple spikes
- Alpine Sunflower in golden yellows
- Alpine Forget-Me-Not and Mountain Bluebells in blues
- Alpine Primrose in pinks
- Yellow Avalanche Lily

Each species has its own unique flower shape and colors!
